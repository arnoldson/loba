Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Loba ("Loba," "we," "us," or "our") is a hyperlocal, anonymous social posting app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Loba, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password (handled securely via our authentication provider, Supabase Auth — we never see or store your password in plain text).

Location data

Loba's core functionality is location-based. When you create a post, react to a post, or comment, we collect your device's precise GPS coordinates at that moment. This location is used to:

  • Anchor your post to a specific location on the map
  • Determine whether you are physically close enough (within 50 meters) to react to or comment on a post
  • Display nearby posts to you as you browse the map

We do not track or store your location continuously in the background — location is only captured at the moment you take an action (posting, reacting, or commenting). Along with the coordinates, your device reports how accurate the reading is and when it was taken, which we use to reject stale or imprecise readings.

User content

We collect the content you create: post text, tags, and comments. This content is publicly visible to anyone browsing that location on the map, for as long as the post remains active (see "Data Retention" below).

IP address

When you use Loba while signed in, we record the IP address your requests come from and link it to your account. We use it to:

  • Detect accounts that may be evading a ban (an account that shares an IP address with a banned account may be limited to view-only access until we review it)
  • Check that the location reported with a post is plausible for the approximate region of your IP address. Posts that don't match are flagged for moderation review; this does not reveal your IP address or location to other users.
  • Limit repeated sign-in attempts, to protect accounts from password guessing

Automatically collected information

Like most apps, our servers automatically log basic technical information (such as IP address and request timestamps) needed to operate the service and diagnose problems.

2. Anonymity — How It Works

Loba is built around anonymity by design:

  • Your account email is never shown publicly and is not attached to your posts, reactions, or comments as visible to other users.
  • Each post you make displays a per-post anonymous name, generated as a one-way (non-reversible) hash derived from your account and that specific post. This means the same anonymous name will appear consistently if you comment multiple times on the same post, but it cannot be used to link your activity across different posts, and it cannot be reversed to reveal your identity or email.

Please note: while your identity is not displayed to other users, Loba retains the underlying account association internally (for example, to prevent you from voting on your own posts, to let you manage and delete your own posts, and to comply with legal obligations if required). Anonymity here refers to what other users can see — it is not a guarantee against disclosure required by law.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate core app functionality (displaying posts, enforcing proximity rules, managing post expiry)
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • To prevent abuse (e.g., detecting self-voting, spam, spoofed locations, ban evasion, or misuse of the reporting system)
  • To review reports of objectionable content and act on them
  • To communicate with you about your account, if necessary

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your information to third parties.

4. How We Share Information

We use a small number of service providers that process data on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes:

  • Supabase — database hosting and authentication
  • Railway — hosting for Loba's server, which receives your app's requests (including your IP address)

The map in the app is provided by Apple Maps. Your device requests map imagery from Apple directly, under Apple's own privacy policy; we do not send Apple your account information or posts.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or in response to valid legal requests from public authorities.

5. Data Retention

  • Posts expire automatically — 24 hours by default. Upvotes can extend a post's lifespan, up to a maximum of 7 days.
  • After expiry, posts are archived and permanently deleted 30 days later.
  • When you report a post, we keep a copy of the reported content and a record of which account filed the report, so we can review it — even if the post later expires or is deleted.
  • If an account is banned, we keep its email address and associated IP addresses to prevent it from signing up again.
  • You can delete your own posts and comments at any time from the "My Posts" screen.
  • Your account information is retained until you delete your account.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Delete individual posts or comments at any time via the "My Posts" screen.
  • Delete your account at any time from within the app's settings. This permanently deletes your email address, account profile, votes, and IP address records. Posts and comments you made are not deleted immediately: they stay visible under a different anonymous name and are no longer linked to you in any way. They expire and are removed on the normal schedule above. To remove specific content right away, delete it from "My Posts" before deleting your account. Reports you filed are kept for moderation, subject to any legal retention requirements.
  • Location permissions can be managed or revoked at any time via your device settings. Note that Loba requires location access to function, since posts and interactions are location-based.

7. Children's Privacy

Loba is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit and at rest via our infrastructure provider, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Effective date" above when changes are made. Continued use of Loba after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
