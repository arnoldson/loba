Loba Support

Loba is a hyperlocal, anonymous posting app: posts are pinned to the place they were made, and you find them by exploring the map.

Contact us

For help with the app, your account, or a post you're concerned about, email arnold.son@icloud.com. Please include what you were doing and, if it's about a post, roughly where and when you saw it.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Common questions

Why does Loba need my location?

Posts are tied to the place they were made, so Loba needs your location to place a new post, and to check that you're nearby before you vote or comment. Location is only used while the app is open, and your precise coordinates are only sent to Loba when you post, vote, or comment. You can change this any time in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Loba.

Why can't I vote or comment on a post?

You need to be within about 50 meters of a post to vote on it. To comment, you need to be within 50 meters, or to have already voted on that post. If you're close by and it still won't let you, wait a moment for your phone to get a precise location fix, then try again.

How long do posts last?

Posts expire after 24 hours. Each upvote adds time, up to a maximum of 7 days. You can delete your own posts at any time from the My Posts tab.

Is Loba really anonymous?

Other users never see your email or a username. Each post shows a random anonymous name that's different for every post, so your activity can't be linked across posts. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what we store.

How do I report a post?

Open the post on the map and tap the 🚩 flag, then choose a reason. We review reports and remove content that breaks the Terms of Service. You can also email us with details.

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam or junk folder, and make sure you signed up with the right address. Tapping the link in the email confirms your account; then return to Loba and sign in.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account. Posts and comments you made stay visible under a different anonymous name until they expire, and are no longer linked to you. To remove them right away, delete them from My Posts first.
